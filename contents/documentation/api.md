# STRIDERA — REST API Reference (All 7 Roles)

Base URL: `http://localhost:4000`

## 1. Authentication & Account (`/api/auth`)
- `POST /api/auth/register` — Register customer account (`FR-1.1`)
- `POST /api/auth/register-seller` — Register merchant application with `identificationDetails` (`FR-1.2`)
- `POST /api/auth/login` — Authenticate any of the **7 roles** (`FR-1.3`)
- `POST /api/auth/logout` — Clear session cookie (`FR-1.10`)
- `GET /api/auth/me` — Get current user profile & addresses
- `PUT /api/auth/profile` — Update name, phone, avatar (`FR-1.6`)
- `PUT /api/auth/password` — Change password (`FR-1.7`)
- `POST /api/auth/forgot-password` & `POST /api/auth/reset-password` — Password recovery (`FR-1.7`)
- `GET /api/auth/addresses`, `POST /api/auth/addresses`, `DELETE /api/auth/addresses/:id` — Saved addresses (`FR-1.8`)

## 2. Catalog, Filters & Reviews (`/api/products`)
- `GET /api/products` — Multi-seller catalog (`category`, `brand`, `seller`, `size`, `color`, `minPrice`, `maxPrice`, `search`, `sort`, `featured`) (`FR-2.1`–`FR-2.7`)
- `GET /api/products/categories` & `GET /api/products/brands` — Categories & brands
- `GET /api/products/:slug` — Product details, variants, seller info, and reviews (`FR-2.8`–`FR-2.10`)
- `POST /api/products/:slug/reviews` — Submit product review & seller rating (`FR-2.11`–`FR-2.12`)

## 3. Orders & Multi-Seller Slicing (`/api/orders`)
- `POST /api/orders` — Create order, slice into `SellerOrder`s, and deduct variant stock atomically (`FR-3.7`–`FR-3.9`)
- `GET /api/orders` — Customer order history (`FR-3.10`)
- `GET /api/orders/:orderNumber` — Order detail with seller slices, payments, and shipments (`FR-3.10`)
- `PATCH /api/orders/:orderNumber/confirm-receipt` — Shopper confirms delivery receipt (`FR-3.11`)
- `PATCH /api/orders/:orderNumber/cancel` — Shopper cancels pre-shipment order & restores stock (`FR-3.12`)

## 4. E-Wallet Payments (`/api/payments`)
- `POST /api/payments/create-session` — Create `gcash`, `maya`, or `paypal` session (`FR-4.1`–`FR-4.6`)
- `POST /api/payments/confirm-sandbox` — Authorize sandbox e-wallet QR payment (`FR-4.7`)
- `POST /api/payments/fail-sandbox` — Simulate failed e-wallet payment for retry testing (`FR-4.9`)
- `POST /api/payments/webhook` — Idempotent HMAC-verified webhook handler (`FR-4.8`)
- `POST /api/payments/simulate` — Admin demo payment fallback (`FR-4.12`)

## 5. Role 2: Seller Portal (`/api/seller`)
- `GET /api/seller/dashboard` — Store KPIs, gross/commission/net earnings, and payouts (`FR-5.1`, `FR-5.13`)
- `PUT /api/seller/profile` — Update store profile & identification details (`FR-5.2`)
- `GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /api/seller/products` & `POST /api/seller/products/:id/variants` — Product & SKU CRUD (`FR-5.3`–`FR-5.8`)
- `GET /api/seller/orders` — Seller order slices (`FR-5.9`)
- `PATCH /api/seller/orders/:id/accept` — Accept & prepare order slice (`FR-5.10`)
- `PATCH /api/seller/orders/:id/fulfill` — Mark shipped, attach courier/waybill, and create/update `Shipment` (`FR-5.11`–`FR-5.12`)
- `PATCH /api/seller/orders/:id/cancel` — Cancel slice & restore stock (`FR-5.12`)

## 6. Role 4: Inventory Manager Portal (`/api/inventory`)
- `GET /api/inventory/dashboard` — Stock KPIs & low-stock alerts (`FR-6.1`, `FR-6.5`)
- `GET /api/inventory/variants` — Filterable SKU inventory table (`FR-6.2`)
- `POST /api/inventory/incoming` — Log incoming restock (`FR-6.3`)
- `POST /api/inventory/outgoing` — Log outgoing stock dispatch (`FR-6.4`)
- `PATCH /api/inventory/variants/:id/adjust` — Manual stock count adjustment (`FR-6.6`, `FR-6.7`)
- `PATCH /api/inventory/variants/:id/availability` — Toggle SKU availability (`FR-6.8`)
- `GET /api/inventory/movements` — Stock movement audit log (`FR-6.9`)
- `GET /api/inventory/report` — Per-seller stock & valuation report (`FR-6.10`)

## 7. Role 5: Delivery Personnel Portal (`/api/delivery`)
- `GET /api/delivery/shipments` — Courier shipment manifest & customer delivery details (`FR-7.1`–`FR-7.3`)
- `PATCH /api/delivery/shipments/:id/accept` — Accept delivery assignment (`FR-7.4`)
- `PATCH /api/delivery/shipments/:id/collect` — Confirm package pickup from seller (`FR-7.5`)
- `PATCH /api/delivery/shipments/:id/status` — Update transit status & record Proof of Delivery (`FR-7.6`–`FR-7.9`)
- `POST /api/delivery/shipments/:id/fail` — Record failed delivery attempt or return-to-seller (`FR-7.10`)

## 8. Role 6: Customer Support Portal (`/api/support`)
- `POST /api/support/tickets` & `GET /api/support/my-tickets` — Customer ticket submission & tracking (`FR-8.1`–`FR-8.2`)
- `GET /api/support/tickets` — Support ticket queue (`FR-8.3`)
- `GET /api/support/orders/lookup` — Diagnostic lookup of orders, payments, and shipments (`FR-8.5`–`FR-8.6`)
- `POST /api/support/tickets/:id/messages` — Reply to ticket thread (`FR-8.4`)
- `PATCH /api/support/tickets/:id/status` — Update ticket status (`FR-8.10`)
- `POST /api/support/tickets/:id/action` — Approve cancellation/return or escalate refund to Finance (`FR-8.7`–`FR-8.9`)

## 9. Role 7: Finance Officer Portal (`/api/finance`)
- `GET /api/finance/dashboard` — Gross revenue, `5%` commission, net seller earnings, payments, payouts, and refund queue (`FR-9.1`–`FR-9.7`)
- `PATCH /api/finance/payments/:id/verify` — Verify e-wallet payment reference (`FR-9.3`)
- `POST /api/finance/payouts` — Disburse seller payout (`FR-9.8`)
- `POST /api/finance/refunds/:ticketId/process` — Process approved customer refund (`FR-9.9`)
- `GET /api/finance/reports` — Executive financial summary report (`FR-9.10`)

## 10. Role 3: Administrator Portal (`/api/admin`)
- `GET /api/admin/reports` — Executive platform analytics (`FR-10.1`)
- `GET /api/admin/users`, `PATCH /api/admin/users/:id/role`, `PATCH /api/admin/users/:id/status` — 7-role user governance (`FR-10.2`, `FR-10.4`)
- `GET /api/admin/sellers`, `PATCH /api/admin/sellers/:id/status`, `PATCH /api/admin/sellers/:id/brand` — Seller verification & brand assignment (`FR-10.3`, `FR-10.5`)
- `GET/PUT /api/admin/platform-config` — Editable platform settings (`FR-10.9`)
- `GET /api/admin/config-status`, `GET /api/admin/payment-logs`, `GET /api/admin/audit-logs` — Read-only API audit & logs (`FR-10.6`–`FR-10.10`)
